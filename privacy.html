Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.irkprotocol

Short version: Your training log stays on your iPhone. There is no account. A Google banner ad supports the app, and Irk does not track you across other companies' apps.

1. Stored on your iPhone

The app saves this on the device:

  • Check-ins, water, meals, movement snacks, and completed sessions
  • Your attitude, wake time, and which protocols are on
  • Field-run distance and route from a sortie you started
  • Your athlete profile, score, and unlocked kit

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes this save unless it is in a device backup.

2. Camera, location, and motion

  • The camera counts shuttle touches and jump squats. Video is not recorded or saved.
  • GPS runs only while a field sortie is active, including if the screen locks during that run.
  • Motion is read during a sortie so a car or bike does not count as a run.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Irk schedules the nags you turned on. You can turn nags off in the app or in iOS Settings.

4. Ads

Irk shows a banner ad from Google AdMob so the app can stay free. Ad requests are non-personalized. Google may receive a device identifier and basic ad-interaction data to deliver the ad and limit how often it appears. Irk does not send Google your workouts, check-ins, routes, or score. The banner hides while a nag is on screen and while a workout, drill, or field run is open.

Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The developer site also publishes app-ads.txt for this AdMob publisher.

5. What this app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No sale of your training log
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps by Irk
  • No health-record upload and no medical diagnosis
